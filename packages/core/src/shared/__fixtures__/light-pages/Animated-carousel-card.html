Animated carousel card

A coverflow carousel built from the design-system Carousel, Card, Badge, Tag, Avatar, and Button. The centre card rests at full scale while its neighbours scale, rotate back into depth, and dissolve into the page. Drag left or right, use the indicators, or use the keyboard — autoplay advances on its own and steps back when it reaches the last card.
